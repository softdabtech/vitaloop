# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 19, 2026

VITALOOP ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our AI-powered blood test analysis platform.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Health Data
- **Lab Test PDFs**: Blood test reports you upload for analysis
- **Biomarker Data**: Values, reference ranges, and status extracted from your labs
- **Protocol Information**: Personalized supplement recommendations we generate
- **Progress Data**: Weekly check-ins, symptom tracking, and biomarker trends over time

### 1.2 Account Information
- Email address (for login and communication)
- Password (encrypted and hashed)
- Account creation and last login dates
- Subscription plan and billing status

### 1.3 Usage Data
- Pages visited and features used
- Time spent in the application
- Device type and browser information
- IP address and general location (city/country level)

### 1.4 Payment Information
We use Stripe for payment processing. We **never** store your full credit card number or financial details on our servers. Stripe handles all payment data according to PCI DSS standards.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 To Provide Our Service
- Analyze your lab reports using AI
- Generate personalized health protocols
- Track your biomarker progress over time
- Send check-in reminders (if you enable notifications)
- Provide customer support

### 2.2 To Improve Our Product
- Understand which features are most helpful
- Identify and fix bugs
- Improve AI analysis accuracy
- Develop new features based on user needs

### 2.3 To Communicate With You
- Send transactional emails (welcome, password reset, billing)
- Notify you of important updates to our service
- Respond to your questions and support requests

### 2.4 What We DO NOT Do

We **NEVER**:
- ❌ Sell your health data to third parties
- ❌ Share your information with advertisers or data brokers
- ❌ Use your data for AI model training without explicit consent
- ❌ Share your data with insurance companies
- ❌ Share your data with employers or healthcare providers without your permission
- ❌ Use your data for purposes beyond providing our service

## 3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

### 3.1 Data Storage
- **Location**: All data is stored on secure servers hosted by DigitalOcean in the United States
- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption
- **Access Control**: Database access is restricted to essential services only
- **Backups**: Regular encrypted backups for disaster recovery

### 3.2 PDF File Handling
- **Temporary Processing**: Lab PDFs are processed for analysis only
- **Not Stored Permanently**: PDFs are **deleted immediately after analysis** completes
- **You Control Re-uploads**: You can re-upload your labs anytime

### 3.3 Security Measures
- **HTTPS/SSL Encryption**: All data transfers use industry-standard encryption
- **Password Security**: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (never stored in plain text)
- **Multi-Factor Authentication**: Available for added account security
- **Session Management**: Automatic logout after period of inactivity
- **Regular Security Audits**: Third-party security assessments
- **Firewall Protection**: Network-level security measures
- **Intrusion Detection**: Monitoring for unauthorized access attempts

## 5. Your Rights and Control

### 5.1 Access and Portability
- **View Your Data**: Access all your biomarker data, protocols, and account information anytime
- **Export Your Data**: Download your complete health data as CSV or PDF
- **Request Copy**: Email us to receive a complete copy of all data we hold about you

### 5.2 Correction and Updates
- **Update Profile**: Change your email, password, or personal information in Settings
- **Correct Data**: Report any errors in biomarker data extraction for correction

### 5.3 Data Deletion
- **Delete Account**: Remove your account and all associated data from Settings
- **Deletion Timeline**: All data permanently deleted within 30 days
- **What Gets Deleted**: Biomarkers, protocols, check-ins, account info, all personal data
- **What Remains**: Aggregated, anonymized analytics (cannot be linked back to you)

### 5.4 Communication Preferences
- **Opt Out of Emails**: Unsubscribe from reminder emails (transactional emails cannot be disabled)
- **Notification Settings**: Control which emails you receive in Settings

### 5.5 Right to Object
You can object to certain data processing activities. Contact us at privacy@vitaloop.today to exercise this right.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Inactive Accounts**: Accounts inactive for 24 months may be deleted with 30 days notice
